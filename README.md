# Assassin's Creed Black Flag Checkers Solver

Unofficial browser-based solver for the **Checkers** tavern mini-game in **Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag**.

Set up the board, choose your side, click the opponent's move, and the solver suggests a reply. The app runs in the browser and does not require a server.

## Features

- 8x8 playable board with coordinates.
- Board orientation follows the side you play.
- Position editor for men and kings.
- Click-based move entry, including multi-captures.
- Automatic suggested move after the opponent's move.
- Visible arrow for the suggested move.
- Undo for the last move.
- English, French, and Spanish interface.
- Optional board color inversion.

## Game Rules Implemented

This is **not Russian draughts**. It follows the custom rules observed in the mini-game:

- Men move forward diagonally by one square.
- Men capture forward only.
- Kings are not flying kings.
- Kings move one square diagonally.
- Kings capture by a short jump forward or backward.
- Captures are mandatory.
- Multi-captures are supported.
- Promotion during a capture chain is supported.

## Use It

Open the published page or `index.html` in a browser.

1. Choose your language and side.
2. Recreate the current board position.
3. Choose who plays now.
4. Start the assistant.
5. Click the piece that moved, then its landing square.
6. For a capture chain, click every landing square in order.
7. When the solver shows an arrow, play that move in the real game, then click the same move in the app.

## Solver Strength

The suggested move is generated by a small local minimax search with a simple evaluation function. It is not random, but it is also not a tournament-grade checkers engine. It is meant as a practical helper for this specific mini-game.
